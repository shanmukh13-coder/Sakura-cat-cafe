The cafe

A living room,
borrowed by cats.

Sakura Cat Cafe is a small cat-lounge cafe tucked into Kingston Avenue, built for people who want their coffee break to come with a little company. Soft seating, filtered light, and a rotating cast of resident cats who mostly do what they like.

We keep things simple: good drinks, a calm room, and cats who are looked after properly — fed on schedule, given space when they want it, and never woken up for a photo.

Before you visit

House rules

Simple courtesies that keep the cats comfortable and the room calm for everyone.

01

Let them come to you

Cats choose who they sit with. Please don't chase, corner, or pick one up uninvited.

02

No flash photography

Regular photos are welcome — just keep the flash off for the cats' comfort.

03

Food stays with you

Please don't feed the cats from your plate. Treats are available at the counter.

04

Indoor voices only

It's a small room and a napping cat's dream. We keep the volume low throughout.
